Standing orders

Keeping the light

  • Things come out of the sea and crawl toward the lighthouse. They can only move in the dark.
  • Anything inside the beam freezes and slowly burns away. Focusing the beam narrows it and burns far hotter, until the lens overheats.
  • Rocks and the big ones cast shadows. Whatever hides in a shadow keeps moving.
  • Survive until dawn. Spend the embers you collect on lanterns, reflectors and better lamp parts.
