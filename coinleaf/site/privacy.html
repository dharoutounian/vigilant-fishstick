Privacy Policy

Coinleaf · Last updated November 6, 2026

The short version

Coinleaf does not collect any data. Everything you enter stays on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

What Coinleaf stores, and where

The transactions, categories, budgets, savings goals, repeating transactions and settings you enter are saved only in Coinleaf’s private storage on your iPhone. We, the developer, never receive or have access to any of it.

Like other app data, it is included in your iCloud or computer backups of your device if you have backups turned on. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

Network access

Coinleaf works entirely offline and makes no network requests of its own. The only time it goes online is when you tap Privacy policy or Help & support in Settings, which opens these web pages.

Face ID and Touch ID

If you turn on App Lock, Coinleaf asks iOS to confirm it’s you. Face ID and Touch ID are handled entirely by iOS: Coinleaf is only told whether the check succeeded and never sees or stores any biometric data.

Exports

When you choose Export to spreadsheet, Coinleaf creates a CSV file on your device and opens the iOS share sheet. Where that file goes next is your choice.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t collect personal information, financial information, contacts, location, identifiers or usage data.
  • We don’t use analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking services.
  • We don’t connect to your bank or any financial account.
  • We don’t sell or share data, because we don’t have any.

Deleting your data

Open Coinleaf, go to Settings › Erase all data. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored on the device.

Children

Coinleaf collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If Coinleaf ever starts collecting data, this page will say so before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy? See the support page.
