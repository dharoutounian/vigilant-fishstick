Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. Coinleaf has no accounts and no servers. See the privacy policy.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Coinleaf’s data is part of your normal device backup. Restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one and your Coinleaf data comes with it. You can also export everything to a spreadsheet from Settings › Export to spreadsheet (CSV).

How do repeating transactions work?

When you add a transaction, choose Weekly, 2 weeks, Monthly or Yearly under Repeat. Coinleaf adds each one on its date, even if you didn’t open the app that day. A monthly repeat that starts on the 31st falls on the last day of shorter months. Pause, edit or stop repeats in Settings › Repeating transactions.

How do budgets work?

Give any spending category a monthly limit on the Budgets tab. The same limits apply every month. Coinleaf shows what’s left, how much you can spend per day for the rest of the month, and turns a budget amber at 80% and red when it’s over.

Does changing the currency convert my amounts?

No. Currency only changes how amounts are displayed. Coinleaf is designed for tracking money in one currency.

I deleted a category. Where did its transactions go?

Nowhere. A category that has transactions is hidden from the picker but kept on its existing transactions, so your history stays accurate.

How do I turn on Face ID?

Settings › App Lock. Coinleaf then asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode when you open it, and again if you’ve been away for more than 30 seconds.

How do I start over?

Settings › Erase all data removes everything from the device. This can’t be undone.
